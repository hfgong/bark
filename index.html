📱 Share & Install Bark

Bark is a zero-network PWA. You can install it directly to your home screen or share it with other phones.

  • iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share button (box with arrow) → "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the Three dots (⋮) → "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

ℹ️ About Bark

Bark is a 100% client-side, zero-dependency audio soundboard designed specifically for smartphones.

Features:

  • ✈️ 100% Offline: Pre-caches all 16 dog and cat sounds via Service Worker. Works completely in Airplane Mode.
  • 🔊 Instant Touch: Leading silence removed using acoustic RMS detection for zero-latency response.
  • 🎵 Pitch & Loop Control: Modulate vocal tones and loop soothing purrs or guard alerts.
  • 🐕 Dog Whistle & Prank Timer: Built-in ultrasonic generator and delayed surprise triggers.
